Delivery Twin changelog

2026-11-16, v2026.11.16. You can now log from a spot. Dropoff pins can be dragged, and spots have links to rewards.

2026-11-15, v2026.11.15. Added direction chevrons on the delivery lines. Added links that open the RTSS and RDSS site surveys.

2026-11-14, v2026.11.14. Added Driver Chat, which runs on its own backend and now shares the twin chat with Field Twin. Field Twin wording was removed from the app. Dropoff places now have kinds, the companion is hidden, and there is a new Delivery Twin app icon.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Added fog of war spots, then pickups, dropoffs, and delivery lines on the map. Lockers are now hidden, the color key folds away, and there is a link to Field Dispatch. Delivery Twin now has its own inventory app, and an Offers tab replaces Roads.

2026-11-12, v2026.11.12. Renamed the app Delivery Twin and gave it its own data tables. Added an offer logger. Added a food spots layer on the map and a form to add a food spot.

2026-11-11, v2026.11.11. Created Delivery Twin as a copy of Field Twin. The road controls on the map are now hidden.
